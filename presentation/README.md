- [Editable PowerPoint](MiniPLE_Challenge.pptx)
- [PDF for presenting or sharing](MiniPLE_Challenge.pdf)

The presentation is in English, matching the handout. Slides 1–20 introduce the challenge in approximately 20–25 minutes. Slides 21–23 provide optional technical detail and references.

Every PowerPoint slide includes speaker notes with explanations, exact figures, and sources. The loss chart and comparison tables remain editable. The PDF contains the slides only.

The presentation covers all six tracks, the historical results, parameter/token budgets, data reconstruction, fair controls, and expected submissions. Slides 17–18 cover GPU rentals through Vast.ai and Runpod, instance selection, storage, and an illustrative cost calculation. Provider information was checked on 8 November 2026; students must check live prices and availability before renting. Historical results remain clearly separate from experiments on the reconstructed corpus. Course deadlines, team size, GPU allocation, and final grading weights remain instructor decisions.
